Progressive Ethical Practices

Integrity should be reflected in everyday project decisions: what is promised, how information is shared and how responsibilities are exercised.

AI-generated conceptual illustration · Not a company project or team photograph.

Clear commitments

The scope of an appointment, the authority to act and the basis of a commitment should be explicit. We seek to distinguish verified information from assumptions and proposals, so that clients and partners can understand the basis on which a decision is being made.

Responsible business relationships

Relationships with consultants, contractors and suppliers should be assessed for their relevance to the project and the responsibilities they will undertake. Potential conflicts of interest, confidentiality requirements and procurement concerns should be identified and addressed through the appropriate contractual and project procedures.

  • Declare and review potential conflicts before decisions are made.
  • Handle project information according to agreed confidentiality terms.
  • Record material decisions, approvals and changes.

Continuous improvement with accountability

Progressive practice means being willing to question a method and improve it when the evidence supports change. New tools, delivery methods and technologies should be evaluated against practical needs, limitations and responsibilities. Good judgement includes explaining both the opportunity and the conditions required for it to work.

These principles describe the intended approach. Project-specific policies, reporting channels and contractual procedures must be established for the relevant appointment.

What We Do

We connect the thinking behind a development with the technical coordination and delivery structure it requires.

Existing Monarcher Global website imagery · Shown for illustration.

Real estate development

Development begins with a clear proposition for a place. We consider intended use, stakeholder requirements, site conditions and the path from initial concept to an agreed brief. Project viability, planning requirements and delivery options should be assessed using project-specific information and appropriately appointed advisers.

  • Development brief and stakeholder requirements.
  • Coordination of feasibility inputs and project assumptions.
  • A decision framework for scope, priorities and next steps.

Engineering coordination

Engineering turns a brief into coordinated technical requirements. Our focus is on the interfaces between disciplines, the completeness of information and the implications of design choices for construction and operation. The responsibilities of appointed designers, consultants and statutory professionals must remain clearly defined.

  • Discipline and design-interface coordination.
  • Technical query and decision tracking.
  • Alignment of design information with procurement and delivery needs.

Contracting and delivery

A delivery model should match the project. Scope, work packages, programme, quality requirements and authority to make decisions need to be established before construction responsibilities are accepted. Coordination continues through progress reviews, change control, inspections and handover planning.

  • Defined work packages and responsibility boundaries.
  • Programme, cost and progress coordination within scope.
  • Inspection, outstanding-work and handover records.

The precise services, contracting entity, professional responsibilities and deliverables are established in the project proposal and agreement. This overview is not an automatic turnkey commitment.
